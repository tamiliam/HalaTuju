# iPhone walkthrough — Mobile M8 close-out

Use your real iPhone, in Safari, on https://halatuju.xyz. Hold the phone upright.
For each line, tick it or write what is wrong. A screenshot helps.

On EVERY screen, look for these four things:
- The page does not move sideways.
- The text is not cut off, in English, Malay and Tamil.
- Every button is easy to tap with your thumb.
- When you tap a text box, the page does not zoom in.

## 1. Student (use a test student account)
- [ ] Home page: open the menu, then close it.
- [ ] Get started, then sign in. Open the sign-in link from the email. You arrive signed in.
- [ ] Onboarding: exam type, grades, profile. The keyboard does not hide the Next button.
- [ ] Dashboard: the tab bar at the bottom shows. Each tab opens its page.
- [ ] Search: open the filters, pick one, close the filters. The list changes.
- [ ] Open a course. Save it. Open Saved. Compare two courses.
- [ ] Quiz: answer three questions. Go back one question.
- [ ] Scholarship: apply, then open My application and the documents step.
- [ ] Settings: change the language to Tamil, then back. Change the theme.
- [ ] Log out. The tab bar shows the signed-out tabs.

## 2. Sponsor (use a test sponsor account)
- [ ] Sponsor login. Forgot password: the reset email link opens the reset form.

- [ ] Fund a student up to the confirm step. Then CANCEL. Do not confirm.
- [ ] My students: open one student.
- [ ] My Account: language, theme, Log out at the end of the page.

## 3. Reviewer (use your reviewer admin account)

- [ ] Open the menu. Switch language. Log out.

## 4. Org admin (use your org-admin account)
